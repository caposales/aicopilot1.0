Chatbot Widget Test Page

This is a test page to demonstrate the chatbot widget functionality. The chatbot should appear as a floating bubble in the bottom-right corner of the page.

Features to Test:

  • Chatbot bubble appears in bottom-right corner
  • Click bubble to open chat window
  • Send a message and receive AI response
  • Chat window can be closed and reopened
  • Widget styling matches configuration

Sample Questions to Try:

  • "What are your business hours?"
  • "Do you offer free shipping?"
  • "Hello, I need help with my order"
  • "Can you tell me about your return policy?"

The chatbot has been configured with knowledge about business hours (9am-5pm Monday to Friday) and should provide helpful responses based on this information.
